## 1. What this is, and what it is not

The model is a structured set of mechanisms with stated parameters. It produces scenarios: differences between a world with continuing AI progress and a world in which frontier AI stopped improving in 2023. Its ranges are ranges over its own assumptions. They are not forecasts, not forecast intervals, and not statements about what will happen to any person, occupation, company, region or economy.
